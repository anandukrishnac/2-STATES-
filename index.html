About The 2 States Event

The Department of Hotel and Catering Management at Asan Memorial College presents 2 STATES—a unique culinary and cultural celebration designed, cooked, and managed by 2nd-year hospitality students.

Islamic Buffet Style

A practical, efficient, and aesthetically pleasing serving concept. 100% Halal compliant, time efficient, reduced waiting times, and elegant presentation.

Traditional Service Attire

Servers dressed in authentic attire—Punjabi Kurta & Turbans and Tamil Kanchipuram Silk Sarees—bringing deep cultural immersion to your table.
